# ElevenLabs voice design — Nascence

Direction from the client: girlish and lively (closer in spirit to a bright, playful anime heroine than to a calm,
ethereal narrator). We describe the qualities only; no named character or voice actor is referenced or cloned.

Prompt (Voice Design):
A bright, youthful teenage girl's voice speaking clear, natural Standard Mandarin. Light, sweet, clear timbre with a
medium-high pitch. Lively, energetic and cheerful, with a playful, teasing sing-song lilt, quick bouncy rhythm and
little upward flicks at the end of phrases; she sounds like she is smiling. Can drop to a soft, gentle, slightly
mysterious tone for quiet lines. Expressive anime-style delivery, natural, not shrill, not babyish, not nasal.
Studio-quality, close microphone, no reverb, no background noise.
