# Flow video prompts (frames mode, 16:9, 720p, x1)

Common suffix (all clips): "Static locked camera, no zoom, no pan. The flat chroma-key green background stays
perfectly uniform and unchanged. Keep the exact character design, colors, outline and chibi sticker art style from
the frame. Smooth limited 2D animation like a game desktop-pet sprite. No new objects, no text."

## Idle (start = end = master, loop)
She stands in place with a serene gentle closed-mouth smile, breathing softly (shoulders and chest rise and fall
slightly), blinks once slowly, her silver hair and the jacket hem sway very gently, tiny bob of the head.

## Batch 3 — idle variants + scene clips (start = end = master; prefix every prompt with
"Only this one character is in the scene; no other people, no other hands.")

### Idle_Star (random idle variant)
She slowly raises one hand to chest height, palm up; a tiny white four-pointed star sparkle forms above her fingertip
and twinkles; she gazes at it with a serene, gentle closed-mouth smile, then the sparkle fades and she lowers her hand.

### Idle_Orbit (random idle variant)
The small floating black-hole orb with its blue-white ring drifts in a slow circle around her; she follows it with her
eyes and turns her head slightly, serene gentle smile, then the orb returns to its starting place.

### Idle_Feather (random idle variant)
A single small white feather drifts down in front of her; she gently catches it in her open palm, looks at it with a
calm, kind smile, then lets it float away out of frame.

### Concern (error / tool fail)
Her serene smile softens into gentle concern: brows lift slightly, eyes look down, she places one hand lightly on the
blue brooch at her chest and tilts her head, then recovers a calm reassuring small smile. Not crying, not slumping.

### Surprise (turn interrupted)
She blinks in mild surprise, eyes widen a little and mouth forms a small soft "o", one hand rises near her cheek, then
she relaxes back into a serene gentle smile. Graceful, not comical.

### Nod (tool success)
She closes her eyes with a serene smile and gives one slow gentle nod; a few tiny white star sparkles twinkle beside
her shoulder; she opens her eyes again calmly.
